# Mythology Guide

This guide provides an overview of the mythological systems and divine guides integrated into the Vortex of Enlightenment project.

## Egyptian Mythology

### Horus
- Role: Guide of Vision and Protection
- Domain: Sky, sun, and kingship
- Responsibilities: Guides users through visual perception challenges and protective aspects
- Integration: Assists in pattern recognition and protective awareness exercises

### Isis
- Role: Guide of Wisdom and Magic
- Domain: Magic, life, and motherhood
- Responsibilities: Guides users through magical understanding and life force exercises
- Integration: Assists in understanding energy flows and magical correspondences

### Maat
- Role: Guide of Truth and Balance
- Domain: Truth, justice, and cosmic order
- Responsibilities: Guides users through balance and truth-seeking exercises
- Integration: Assists in maintaining harmony and ethical alignment

## Norse Mythology

### Odin
- Role: Guide of Wisdom and Knowledge
- Domain: Wisdom, poetry, and magic
- Responsibilities: Guides users through knowledge acquisition and runic understanding
- Integration: Assists in deep learning and pattern recognition
