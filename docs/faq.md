# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Vortex of Enlightenment?
Vortex of Enlightenment is an immersive text-based experience that combines various mythological traditions with behavioral profiling to create a unique personal growth journey.

### How long does it take to complete?
There is no fixed completion time. The experience adapts to your pace and learning style. Most users spend several weeks to months exploring all ponds and unlocking their full potential.

### Can I save my progress?
Yes, your progress is automatically saved after each significant interaction. You can safely quit and return to continue your journey later.

## Gameplay & Mechanics

### How does the profile system work?
The profile system tracks eight dimensions of your interaction patterns and learning style. Your profile is continuously updated based on your choices, responses, and challenge completions.

### What if I'm stuck in a pond?
- Try different approaches to challenges
- Engage more with your guide
- Review your profile for areas of improvement
- Consider exploring other accessible ponds

### How do I unlock new streams?
Streams are unlocked by:
- Completing pond-specific challenges
- Achieving certain profile dimension thresholds
- Making connections between different mythological concepts
- Demonstrating understanding of core teachings

### Can I change my guide?
While each pond has a specific guide, their teaching style and interaction patterns adapt to your needs. Focus on building a relationship with your current guide rather than trying to change them.

## Technical Questions

### What are the system requirements?
- Python 3.8 or higher
- Terminal with UTF-8 support
- 100MB free disk space
- Internet connection (for initial setup only)

### The text appears garbled in my terminal
Make sure your terminal:
- Supports UTF-8 encoding
- Uses a font that supports Unicode characters
- Has the correct locale settings

### How do I report a bug?
1. Check if the issue is already reported in our GitHub issues
2. If not, create a new issue with:
   - Detailed description of the problem
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Your system information
   - Any relevant error messages

## Cultural & Mythological Questions

### Do I need prior knowledge of these mythologies?
No, the system is designed to introduce concepts gradually. Your guide will provide necessary context and explanations as you progress.

### Why these particular mythological systems?
These systems were chosen for their:
- Rich symbolic frameworks
- Complementary perspectives on personal growth
- Universal principles that transcend cultural boundaries
- Deep wisdom traditions that remain relevant

### How accurate are the mythological representations?
We strive for authenticity while making the content accessible. Our representations:
- Are based on scholarly sources
- Respect traditional interpretations
- Maintain cultural context
- Balance accuracy with accessibility

## Progress & Learning

### How do I track my progress?
Use these commands:
- `status` for overall progress
- `profile` for dimension levels
- `map` for pond connections
- `achievements` for completed milestones

### What if I disagree with a teaching?
The system encourages critical thinking and personal interpretation. You can:
- Discuss your perspective with your guide
- Explore alternative viewpoints
- Focus on aspects that resonate with you
- Use disagreements as opportunities for deeper understanding

### Can I reset my progress?
Yes, you can start fresh by:
1. Using the `reset` command
2. Confirming your choice
3. Starting a new journey with different choices
